Dear Editors of Physical Review Letters,

Please find enclosed our manuscript titled "Universal Wigner Negativity for Pure Spin States via \pi-Rotation Orthogonality" for consideration as a Letter in Physical Review Letters.

Wigner negativity is a fundamental signature of quantum nonclassicality and a resource for quantum advantage. On the flat phase space R^2, Hudson's theorem famously identifies Gaussian pure states as the unique pure states with non-negative Wigner functions. On the spherical phase space S^2 of spin-j systems, however, Davis, Kumari, Mann, and Ghose [Phys. Rev. Research 3, 033134 (2021)] and Davis, Hennigar, Mann, and Ghose [J. Phys. A 56, 265302 (2023)] conjectured that no pure spin state can have a globally non-negative Stratonovich-Weyl Wigner function (Open Quantum Problem F18). Very recently, Klimov et al. (2026) and Kam (2026) showed that on the negatively curved SU(1,1) hyperboloid, Wigner-positive pure states form a strictly larger set than coherent states, highlighting that settling the positively curved SU(2) conjecture would complete the classification of Hudson's theorem across all constant-curvature homogeneous phase spaces.

In this Letter, we resolve this conjecture in full generality for all spins j in (1/2)N, all pure states, and all 2^{2j} self-dual SU(2)-covariant Stratonovich-Weyl kernels. The proof uncovers a simple geometric theorem of independent interest: every pure spin-j state (j >= 1/2) admits a unit axis u_* on S^2 whose \pi-rotation maps the state to an orthogonal state. For half-integer spins this follows from antipodal oddness; for integer spins it follows from a short divergence-theorem proof that every complex spherical harmonic of degree j >= 1 has a zero, combined with an exact equatorial integral identity relating the average of \pi-rotations on a great circle to the m = 0 Dicke projector.

Because the proof is short, self-contained, and resolves a prominent open problem connecting quantum foundations, phase-space geometry, and spin nonclassicality, we believe it is ideally suited for Physical Review Letters. To ensure airtight mathematical rigor, the entire proof chain has been machine-verified with zero gaps in Lean 4 (4,083 lines, standard Lean axioms only) and cross-checked numerically; all code and proofs are permanently archived on Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24563264) and GitHub (https://github.com/vexpaer/su2-wigner-negativity).

This manuscript has been archived as a preprint on Zenodo (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24563264) and is not under consideration by any other journal.
