Hudson's theorem characterizes non-negative Wigner functions on flat phase space, yet its counterpart on the curved phase space S^2 of spin-j systems remained open since the 2021 conjecture of Davis et al. (Open Quantum Problem F18). Following recent breakthroughs on the negatively curved SU(1,1) hyperboloid, we resolve the positively curved SU(2) case in full generality: no pure spin-j state (j >= 1/2) has a non-negative spherical Wigner function. Our proof reveals universal pi-rotation orthogonality for all pure spin states, completes the constant-curvature trichotomy for quantum nonclassicality, and is completely machine-verified in Lean 4.
